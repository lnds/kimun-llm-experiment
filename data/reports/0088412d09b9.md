In the board (column) view, pressing `↑` or `k` does nothing: the highlighted story never moves up within a column, and pressing it on the first story does not wrap to the last one. `↓`/`j` work, and so do `←`/`→` between columns, so the only way to go back to a story above is to press `↓` until the selection wraps around. In the list view (`v`) `↑` works normally.
