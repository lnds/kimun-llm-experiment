Creating a worktree for a story always reports failure: `sc-cli branch 42 --default --worktree` prints "❌ Failed to create worktree: Failed to create worktree 'sc-42-...' at '../sc-42-...': Preparing worktree (new branch 'sc-42-...')" and exits with an error. The worktree directory and the branch are there afterwards, though, and the story stays in its old state instead of moving to In Progress. In a bare repository the TUI shows the same failure after choosing "Create Worktree".
